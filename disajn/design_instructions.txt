## ALU instructions - 11 ops - 001xx0xx
- 8 symbolizes an 8 bit operation
- 16 symbolizes a 16 bit operation, using Hi from bridge
- if the operation only requires 1 number, than the number is taken from bridgeA and bridgeAHi, otherwise it is bridgeA + bridgeB
- a 16 bit operation is performed on the 8 bit counter like this: counts the non Hi bridges, than uses the carry (in addition), where it stores 1 bit of info, than it wires this carry into the first carry bit of the adder
- in non carry bit operations, the carry bit is not necessary and it is performed as 2 disticnt operations
